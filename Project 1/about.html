Our Focus for the
Fall 2026 Semester

We want to introduce game dvelopment to a larger audience on campus.
This Fall, we want to invite students of all majors to explore how talents outside
of Computer Science are essential to the Game Development Process.

Our Mission

Game Development depends on many disciplines to succeed. Whether you study Computer Sciece, Art, Music, or Writing, your talents serve a vital role in the process of Video Game Development.

Our goal is to clarify the process of Video Game Development in order to make it accessible, collaborative, and fun for everyone on campus. By doing so, we hope to bring together students of different majors in a creative and unique way.

Our Plan for the Fall

Guest Speakers

We will be inviting guest speakers with experience in the Game Development industry. Students, staff, and alumni will offer advice and give an insight into what the field is like.

Collaborative Events

Interactve meetings where students can discuss and present ideas about game concepts, club activities, and more!

VGD Club Game Jam

Semester event where students can participate solo or in groups to create a video game in a one week timespan!
